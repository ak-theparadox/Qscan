Free QR Code Scanner Online

Qscan is a free online QR code scanner that lets you scan QR codes and barcodes directly from your web browser. You can use your device camera to scan a code instantly or upload an image containing a QR code.

No separate QR scanner app is required. Qscan is designed to work on supported mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

How to Scan a QR Code Online

  1. Open the QR scanner and allow camera access.
  2. Point your camera at the QR code.
  3. Keep the QR code clearly visible inside the scanning area.
  4. Wait for Qscan to detect the code.
  5. View or copy the scanned result.

Scan a QR Code From an Image

If you already have a QR code saved as a picture, you don't need another device to scan it. Use the upload button above and select an image containing the QR code. Qscan will try to read the code directly from the uploaded image.
